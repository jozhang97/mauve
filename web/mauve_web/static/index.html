    ALL GENOMES
    Gene comparisons & core/pan genome
    
          
    
            
    Compare complete circular chromosomes using gene families inferred from the Mauve alignment. Includes every loaded genome, regardless of track visibility or zoom.
    
            
    
              
              
              
              
            
    
            
    Import GenBank annotations for every genome to begin.
    
            
    Definitions and matching rules
              
    Protein-coding CDS loci and RNA genes meeting the selected evidence policy are counted once per locus. Pseudogenes, fuzzy locations, and features without known orientation are excluded. Experimental RNA evidence means a nonempty GenBank /experiment qualifier. Reimport older annotations to retain evidence and chromosome topology.
    
              
    Gene matches require the same feature type, compatible orientation in the alignment, the selected fraction of both genes covered by aligned non-gap bases, and the selected identity among aligned A/C/G/T pairs. Matches are grouped transitively into families; unmatched loci form individual families. These are alignment-derived families, not an independent protein-clustering analysis. Missing annotations or missed alignments can inflate the pan genome and reduce the core. The defaults follow the original Mauve exporter's thresholds, but this implementation uses XMFA columns rather than its backbone filtering.
    
              
    Content Jaccard is shared families / distinct families in the pair. Actual adjacency Jaccard compares distinct strand-aware family-end neighbor pairs before removing unshared genes; duplicated pair types count once. Shared-gene conservation is 2 × preserved pairs / (neighbor pairs in A + neighbor pairs in B), after removing unshared families and families with multiple copies in either genome. DCJ uses the same unique shared genes and equals genes minus cycles in the circular breakpoint graph. Empty denominators are unavailable, not zero.
    
              
    Every record is a separate circular chromosome; there are no cross-record adjacencies. Known linear records are rejected; records without topology are assumed circular. Circular-origin genes are ordered across the origin, and overlapping genes are ordered by forward start coordinate. Rotating or reverse-complementing a whole chromosome does not change the metrics.

    Core / pan genome
    
              
    Core: present in every genome. Accessory: present in fewer than all genomes. Genome-specific families are part of the accessory genome. Counts depend on matching thresholds and annotation coverage.
